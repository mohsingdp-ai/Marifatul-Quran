Privacy Policy

App: Quran Urdu Audio (package com.mohsingdp.marifatulquran)
Last updated: 14 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Quran Urdu Audio mobile application (the “App”) handles information. We respect your privacy. The App is designed to work without collecting any personal information about you.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. The App has no user accounts, no login, and no registration. We do not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking of any kind.

Data stored on your device

The following data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to us or to any third party:

  • Your app settings (for example, playback speed, after-ruku behavior, and the WhatsApp-share toggle).
  • Your listening history, memorization progress, and daily-goal preferences, if you use those features.
  • Quran audio recordings you choose to download for offline listening.

If you have enabled Android’s automatic backup, Android may back up your settings and progress to your own Google account. This backup is handled entirely by Google under your Google account settings; we have no access to it.

Internet access

The App uses the internet only to download Quran audio recordings from our public content site over a secure (HTTPS) connection. No personal data is sent during this process.

Permissions

  • Internet — to download audio recordings.
  • Foreground service — to keep audio playback and downloads running reliably.
  • Notifications — to show playback controls and, if you enable it, an optional daily reminder. All notifications are generated locally on your device.

Sharing

When you choose to share a recording (for example, to WhatsApp), the sharing happens through Android’s standard share feature and is initiated only by you. We are not involved in and do not receive anything from that action.

Children’s privacy

The App is suitable for users of all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
